PDF Finder

Upload a CSV of article references to generate consistent filenames and quick links for locating full-text PDFs. Open access PDFs are looked up automatically via the Unpaywall API when a DOI and email address are provided.

Adapted from Full Text Finder by Noah Schroeder.

How it works

  • The CSV should have a header row. Recognised columns (case insensitive): title, authors, doi and year. Only title is required, but a DOI gives the best results.
  • Authors may be separated by semicolons, e.g. Smith, John; Doe, Jane. Suggested filenames use the first two surnames and the year, e.g. SmithDoe2021.pdf.
  • Unpaywall requires an email address with each request. It is sent only to api.unpaywall.org and is not stored anywhere else.
  • Browsers cannot save files to a chosen folder automatically. Copy the suggested filename, open the PDF, and save it manually.
Without an email, DOI and Google Scholar links are still provided but open access PDFs are not looked up.
